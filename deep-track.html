The Deep Track

15 first-principles deep dives that teach each idea by solving a real game. Each starts from a plain situation, builds the concept in everyday words, and shows it on a small program we actually ran — finding a game's Nash equilibria, watching defectors get out-competed by reciprocity, computing an auction's revenue — with computed numbers, an honest limit, and where the idea runs the real world.

Themes And Subthemes

Choice Under Tradeoffs

Before strategy enters, the course needs a language for ranking outcomes and measuring what a person is trying to get.

  • Ranking Outcomes: Preferences, Utility, Expected Utility

Strategic Dependence

The central problem is that one person's best move depends on what other people do.

  • Tables, Trees, And Strategic Dependence: Strategic Form Games, Extensive Form Games
  • Eliminating Bad Moves: Dominance, Iterated Deletion, Rationalizability

Equilibrium As Mutual Consistency

A prediction must be stable against each player's own incentive to change.

  • Stable Predictions: Best Response, Nash Equilibrium, Mixed Strategies
  • Conflict And Security: Zero-Sum Games, Minimax

Time, Threats, And Credibility

Promises and threats matter only when they remain optimal at the moment they must be carried out.

  • Credible Time Paths: Backward Induction, Subgame-Perfect Nash Equilibrium, One-Shot Deviation Principle, Bargaining

Repeated Interaction And Cooperation

Repeated relationships can make cooperation possible because today's action changes tomorrow's incentives.

  • The Future As Discipline: Finitely Repeated Games, Infinitely Repeated Games, Folk Theorem, Implicit Cartels

Private Information And Beliefs

People often act without knowing another person's type, value, cost, or information.

  • Hidden Types And Beliefs: Bayesian Games, Types And Beliefs, Bayesian Nash Equilibrium, Perfect Bayesian Equilibrium

Mechanisms, Auctions, And Markets

Rules shape incentives, so market design asks which rules make strategic behavior produce useful outcomes.

  • Rules For Bidding: Auctions, Revenue Equivalence, Ad Auctions

Communication And Knowledge

Messages matter when they change beliefs, but incentives determine whether messages can be trusted.

  • Messages And Shared Awareness: Signaling, Cheap Talk, Common Knowledge

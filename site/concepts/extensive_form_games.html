Extensive Form Games

A game drawn as a tree so timing and information can be represented.

What real-world problem is this about?

The problem is that some games unfold over time. A later choice can depend on an earlier choice, and some players may know more about the history than others.

Why does this problem exist?

This exists because timing and information change incentives. A threat that sounds good at the start may not be attractive when the threatened moment arrives.

What is the mathematical idea underneath?

Backward induction assigns optimal choices from terminal decision nodes back to the start. The useful relation is `V(node) = max_action V(next node).`. In plain language: V is continuation value; at each decision point the player chooses the next branch with the best continuation value.

Why is this concept important?

This concept matters because it turns a loose strategic story into a checkable claim about what someone would actually choose.

What breaks without it?

Without this concept, the analysis can name the players and choices but cannot tell whether the proposed behavior survives individual incentives. The extra discipline is information sets. Two nodes can be different histories but indistinguishable to the player moving there; that distinction is what later supports signaling and perfect Bayesian equilibrium.

Worked Mini-Example

In an entry game, an incumbent may threaten a price war after entry. The tree asks whether fighting is actually optimal once entry has happened.

Common Misunderstanding

The mistake is to collapse every sequential game into a payoff matrix and lose the credibility problem.

How to Recognize This in a New Paper or Model

Look for histories, decision nodes, information sets, or any claim where the order of moves changes what behavior can be believed.

Connected Concepts

The same primitive returns whenever the course asks whether a proposed behavior is compatible with incentives, beliefs, timing, or information. The tree is the course's way of preventing time from being smuggled into prose. Every branch forces the model to say who moves, what they have seen, and which later choices are actually available.
